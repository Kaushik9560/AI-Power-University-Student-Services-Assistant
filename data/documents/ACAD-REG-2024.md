# 1 Scope and Commencement

1.1 These Regulations govern all Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programmes of the University and apply to all students on the rolls on or after 1 July 2024, irrespective of batch.

1.2 These Regulations supersede the Academic Regulations 2021 in full.

# 2 Definitions

2.1 "Batch" means the year of admission. "Backlog" means a course in which the student has not yet secured a PASS result. "Controller of Examinations" means the officer responsible for the conduct of examinations and declaration of results.

# 3 Credit System and Grading

3.1 Each course carries the credits stated in the programme curriculum. Credits are earned only on a PASS result.

3.2 The Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) are computed on a 10-point scale as the credit-weighted average of grade points.

# 4 Assessment Pattern

4.1 Every theory course is assessed out of 100 marks: internal assessment of 40 marks and an end-semester examination of 60 marks.

4.2 Internal assessment consists of two mid-semester tests (15 marks each) and assignments (10 marks).

# 5 Fee Table

5.1 The following fees apply per course unless revised by a circular of the Finance Office.

| Item | Amount (INR) |
|---|---|
| Supplementary examination fee per course | 1,000 |
| Re-registration fee per credit | 1,500 |
| Revaluation fee per answer script | 500 |
| Late application fee | 300 |

# 6 Registration

6.1 A student must register for courses within the first week of the semester through the student portal.

# 7 Attendance

7.1 Attendance is recorded for every scheduled class. The attendance percentage in a course is the number of classes attended divided by the number of classes held, expressed as a percentage, computed at the end of the teaching period.

7.2 Minimum attendance for end-semester examination. A student must have a minimum attendance of 75 percent in a course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination in that course. A student with attendance below 75 percent shall be detained in that course, recorded as DETAINED, and shall re-register for the course in a subsequent semester.

7.3 Condonation. Subject to clause 7.2, the Dean (Academics) may condone a shortage of attendance of up to 10 percentage points below the prescribed minimum on medical grounds, on production of a certificate from a registered medical practitioner within seven days of the student rejoining classes. Condonation is not available for shortage beyond 10 percentage points.

# 8 Pass Criteria

8.1 A student is declared to have passed a course on securing a minimum of 40 percent of the total marks (40 out of 100) and a minimum of 35 percent in the end-semester examination (21 out of 60). A student who does not satisfy both conditions is declared FAIL and the course becomes a backlog.

8.2 A student who does not appear for the end-semester examination without being detained is recorded as ABSENT and the course becomes a backlog.

# 9 Supplementary Examination

9.1 Eligibility. A student whose regular end-semester result in a course is FAIL or ABSENT is eligible to appear for the supplementary examination in that course. A student recorded as DETAINED in a course is not eligible for the supplementary examination and must re-register for the course under clause 7.2.

9.2 Procedure. To apply for the supplementary examination a student shall: (a) log in to the student portal within 10 working days of the declaration of the regular results; (b) select the courses to be re-examined; (c) pay the supplementary examination fee prescribed in clause 5.1 per course; and (d) download the hall ticket, which is issued three days before the examination. Late applications are accepted up to 5 working days after the deadline on payment of the late application fee.

9.3 Attempts. A student may appear for the supplementary examination in a course in at most 4 supplementary examination sessions following the regular examination.

9.4 Marks. Only the end-semester component is re-examined in the supplementary examination. Internal assessment marks already secured are carried forward. On passing the supplementary examination the result of the course is recorded as PASS and the backlog is cleared on declaration of the result by the Controller of Examinations.

# 10 Promotion

10.1 A student is promoted to the next academic year provided the number of active backlogs does not exceed 4.

# 11 Revaluation

11.1 A student may apply for revaluation of an end-semester answer script within 7 days of the declaration of results on payment of the revaluation fee in clause 5.1.
