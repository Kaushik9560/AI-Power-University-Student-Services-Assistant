# 1 Scope

1.1 These Regulations apply to all B.Tech programmes of the University from 1 July 2021.

# 7 Attendance

7.2 Minimum attendance for end-semester examination. A student must have a minimum attendance of 80 percent in a course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination in that course. Students below 80 percent shall be detained.

7.3 Condonation. The Principal may condone a shortage of attendance of up to 5 percentage points on medical grounds.

# 8 Pass Criteria

8.1 A student passes a course on securing a minimum of 45 percent of the total marks and 40 percent in the end-semester examination.

# 9 Supplementary Examination

9.3 A student may appear for the supplementary examination in a course in at most 3 supplementary sessions.
