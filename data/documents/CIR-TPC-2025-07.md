# 1 Purpose

1.1 This circular sets out the conditions a student must satisfy to register for campus placement drives conducted by the Training and Placement Cell for the 2025-26 placement season and thereafter until revised.

# 2 Eligibility Conditions

2.1 CGPA. The student must have a CGPA of 6.0 or above at the time of registration.

2.2 Backlogs. The student must have no active backlogs. A backlog is treated as cleared only when the Controller of Examinations has declared a PASS result in the supplementary or re-registered examination.

2.3 Attendance. The student must have an aggregate attendance of at least 75 percent in the current semester across all registered courses.

2.4 Discipline. No disciplinary action may be pending against the student.

# 3 Clearing Backlogs During the Season

3.1 A student with active backlogs becomes eligible to register once all backlogs are cleared through the supplementary examination and the results are declared, provided the CGPA condition in clause 2.1 continues to be satisfied. Such students may register for subsequent drives but not for drives already concluded.

# 4 Company-Specific Criteria

4.1 Individual companies may specify stricter criteria, for example a higher CGPA cut-off or a limit on the number of past backlogs. Such criteria are notified with each drive and prevail for that drive only.
