# 1 Revised Condonation Limit

1.1 With effect from 1 October 2026, for students admitted in 2024 or later, the Dean (Academics) may condone a shortage of attendance of up to 5 percentage points below the prescribed minimum on medical grounds. Clause 7.3 of the Academic Regulations 2024 stands superseded to this extent for these batches.
