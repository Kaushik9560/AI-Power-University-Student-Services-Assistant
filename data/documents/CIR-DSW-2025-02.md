# 1 Scholarships Offered

1.1 Merit Scholarship: full tuition fee waiver for students in the top 5 percent of their batch by CGPA, with a minimum CGPA of 8.5 and no backlogs.

1.2 Merit-cum-Means Scholarship: 50 percent tuition fee waiver for students with a CGPA of 7.5 or above and an annual family income below INR 4,00,000.

1.3 Sports Scholarship: INR 25,000 per year for students who have represented the University at inter-university level in the preceding year.

# 2 Application

2.1 Applications are submitted through the student portal within 30 days of the publication of semester results, with supporting documents. Scholarships are awarded for one academic year and renewed subject to continued eligibility.
