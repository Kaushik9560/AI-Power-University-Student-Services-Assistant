# 1 Schedule

1.1 The supplementary examinations for CSE courses of the odd semester 2026 will be held from 20 to 28 October 2026. Hall tickets will be available on the portal from 17 October 2026.

1.2 Students must apply through the portal as per clause 9.2 of the Academic Regulations 2024. The last date for applications without late fee is 10 October 2026.
