# 1 Purpose

1.1 In pursuance of the decision of the Academic Council at its meeting of 20 December 2025, clause 7.2 of the Academic Regulations for B.Tech Programmes 2024 is revised with effect from 15 January 2026.

# 2 Revised Minimum Attendance

2.1 With effect from 15 January 2026, a student must have a minimum attendance of 80 percent in a course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination in that course. A student with attendance below 80 percent shall be detained in that course and shall re-register for the course.

2.2 This clause supersedes clause 7.2 of the Academic Regulations 2024. All other clauses of the Academic Regulations 2024, including the condonation provision in clause 7.3 (up to 10 percentage points below the prescribed minimum on medical grounds), continue to apply.

# 3 Applicability

3.1 This circular applies to all B.Tech programmes and all batches for examinations held on or after the effective date.
