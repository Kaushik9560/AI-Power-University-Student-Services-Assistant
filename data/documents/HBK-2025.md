# 1 How is attendance counted?

Attendance is recorded in every class and shown on the student portal. You need 75 percent attendance in a course to sit the end-semester examination in that course. If you fall short on medical grounds you can apply for condonation at the Dean (Academics) office with your medical certificate.

# 2 What happens if I fail a course?

The course becomes a backlog. You can clear it in the supplementary examination; only the end-semester paper is re-taken and your internal marks are carried forward.

# 3 Can I sit for placements with a backlog?

No. The Training and Placement Cell requires all backlogs to be cleared before you can register for placement drives. See the latest placement circular for the full conditions.

# 4 Hostel

Hostel rooms are allotted each year on a first-come basis through the hostel portal. Mess fees are payable monthly in advance.

# 5 Where do I get my ID card?

New students collect their ID card from the Student Affairs office within two weeks of joining. Replacement cards cost INR 200.
